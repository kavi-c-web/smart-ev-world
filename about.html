What is an EV?

🚗

Electric Power

EVs run completely or partially on electricity using powerful battery systems and electric motors.

🌱

Eco Friendly

Electric vehicles reduce air pollution and help create a cleaner and greener future.

💰

Cost Saving

EVs require less maintenance and lower fuel costs compared to petrol and diesel vehicles.

Types of Electric Vehicles

  • 🔋 Battery Electric Vehicles (BEV) — Fully electric vehicles powered only by batteries.
  • ⚡ Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEV) — Combination of petrol engine and electric motor.
  • 🚙 Hybrid Electric Vehicles (HEV) — Uses both battery and fuel engine without external charging.
